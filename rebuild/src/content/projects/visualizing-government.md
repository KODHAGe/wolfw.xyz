As a part of the State Treasury data analyst team, our mission is to make the wealth of data produced by different processes of government accessible and understandable. Visual exploratory analysis is often a key part in succeeding in this - enabling the translation of data into insight, insight into decisionmaking. And ultimately, better outcomes for us all. Hopefully.

Using methods of UX, UI and data visualization I have produced data-driven products that have been published as part of ministry reports, utilized as tools for human resource development or become parts of the developing https://www.exploreadministration.fi/ -portal.
