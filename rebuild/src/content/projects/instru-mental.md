An instrument of instruments, rain of emoji.

Built with OpenFrameworks, node.js and plain javascript-in-a-browser, Instru-mental allows remote control of sound through any networked, gyroscope or accelerometer equipped device through tilting and moving. The networked device controls the point of gravity in a visualization created in OpenFrameworks, which in turn controls the sound generated, allowing the user a unique interface with which to control the sound. Course project for Multimedia Authoring @ Aalto University.
